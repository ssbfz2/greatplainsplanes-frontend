Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Great Plains Planes ("we," "us") is a personal ADS-B flight tracking digest operated by What Flew Over LLC in Kansas City, Missouri. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

When you sign up for the digest, we collect:

  • Name — used to personalize your emails
  • Email address — used to send you the daily digest
  • Zip code — used to localize flight content to your area

We do not collect payment information, location data beyond your zip code, or any browsing or behavioral data.

How we use it

Your information is used solely to send you the Great Plains Planes daily email digest. We do not use it for advertising, analytics, or any other purpose.

Who we share it with

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties. We use the following services to operate the digest:

  • Amazon Web Services (SES) — email delivery
  • Supabase — subscriber database (hosted in the US)
  • Railway — backend hosting

Each of these services processes your data only as needed to deliver the digest.

Unsubscribing

Every digest email contains a one-click unsubscribe link at the bottom. Clicking it immediately removes you from all future sends. You can also email digest@greatplainsplanes.com to request removal.

Data retention

We retain your information for as long as you are subscribed. If you unsubscribe, your record is marked inactive. You can request complete deletion by emailing us.

Security

Subscriber data is stored in a managed, encrypted database. Email is sent via Amazon SES over TLS. We do not store passwords.

Contact

Questions? Email us at digest@greatplainsplanes.com.
